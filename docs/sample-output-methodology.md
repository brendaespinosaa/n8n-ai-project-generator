# Sample Output — Methodology

## 2.5 METHODOLOGY

The project execution will be organized into strategic action groups based on the resources defined in the budget spreadsheet. Each group connects the planned resources to an operational purpose, ensuring consistency between the budget and the technical execution plan.

### 2.5.1 Provide support materials for planned activities

Support materials will be used to assist the organization and execution of the planned activities. These resources contribute to the operational structure of the project and help ensure that activities are carried out with the necessary basic materials.

### 2.5.2 Structure operational execution conditions

Equipment and operational resources will be used to support the execution environment. These resources provide the necessary structure for carrying out the planned actions in an organized and functional way.

### 2.5.3 Enable practical activities

The practical execution will be supported by the specific resources listed in the budget spreadsheet. These resources will be used according to their stated purpose, ensuring alignment between the planned activities and the available materials.

### 2.5.4 Support technical and administrative management

Administrative, technical, accounting, and legal support will be used to assist project management and compliance. These services help organize the execution process and support the proper use of public resources.

### 2.5.5 Communicate and identify project actions

Communication and visual identity resources will be used to support the identification and dissemination of project actions, according to the purposes described in the budget spreadsheet.
